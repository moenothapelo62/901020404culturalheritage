Traditional Dances

Mokhibo

A graceful kneeling dance by women,using shoulder movements and clapping.

Mohobelo

A powerful male dance involving high kicks and stomping,symbolizing

Motebo

A ceremonial dance perfomed at weddings and traditional gatherings.

Melele

A celebratory dance during feasts or after successful harvests.

Sebota

A men's warrior dance that mimics fighting moves and jumping

Ndlamo

Performed during initiation ,featuring stomping and deep vocal chants

Sehlotho

Social dance at communal events ,includes circle formations and hand movements

Lipatla

A rhythmic group dance with energetic footwork,mostly by youth.

Phathisi

Dance involving ankle rattles or bells ,often done by mem=n.

Tlhobolo

Dance using sticks to stimulate traditional hunting or herding scenes

Letsunyane

A seasonal dance linked to rain-making or harvest rituals.

MUSICAL INSTRUMENTS

Lesiba

A unique mouth-resonated string instrument made from a stick and feather, used by herders.It produces a buzzing sound

Setolo-tolo

A nose flute mode from reed or metal, played by blowing air through the nose.Often used for personal enjoyment.

Mamokhorong

A one-string violin-like instrument played with a bow.Used in folks song and storytelling .

Thomo

A traditionl mouth bow mostly played by women.The mouth acts as a resonator to change picth

Moropa

A drum made from wood and animal skin,played during ceremonies,dances,and gatherings to keep rhythm

Nkoe

A wind imstrument made from animal horn,blown to announce important events or signal people.

Dingolo

Rattles made from seeds or metal ,worn on ankles or wrists during dances to add rhythm.

Khaune

Wooden clappers hit together to keep time during group dances or singing.

Lepatata

A flat percussion board struck with sticks,used in traditional music ensembles

Sebita

SA metal bell or song used to mark time or transitions in rituals or perfmoancs.
